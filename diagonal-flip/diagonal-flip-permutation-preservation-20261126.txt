SPECIFIC PERMUTATION PRESERVATION OF THE DIAGONAL-FLIP c = 128 EXTENSION
26 November 2026

Status: reviewed analytic version for preservation. This note concerns the
specific extension obtained by the diagonal construction followed by the
literal-flip orbifold. It does not replace the separate proof of that basic
construction, and it does not assert a coherent S4 splitting of an
automorphism-group extension.

MAIN CONCLUSION

Assume the complex VOA W and the fixed-point VOA U supplied by Hohn's
Hexagon 2611.00631v2, with the ordinary VOA and module properties stated
below. Let X be the full diagonal extension of U tensor U, and let Y be the
integral cyclic orbifold of X by its literal flip.

Put M = W tensor W tensor W tensor W and

  K = diagonal(A) x P,
  A = O_2(Aut(W)) = (Z/2)^27,
  P = {1, (12)(34), (13)(24), (14)(23)}.

Then Y is a full K-orbifold of M: as an extension of N = M^K, its support
is a maximal totally singular subspace transverse to the untwisted charge
subspace K*. Its SPECIFIC support is generated by the intrinsic N-module
labels containing the unique weight-four ground lines of M(a,p), for
a in A and 1 != p in P. Consequently every factor permutation in S4
preserves this support. Each such permutation of N therefore lifts to an
automorphism of Y.

An order-three factor permutation has a lift of exact order three. It
conjugates the original cyclic-orbifold quantum involution to a distinct
quantum involution. Thus this lift lies outside the centralizer of the
original quantum involution.

These statements concern this actual diagonal-flip extension. No equality
of graded characters is used to infer a VOA isomorphism. No KRR anomaly
assumption, identification with a dense KRR orbifold, or full E8(2)
automorphism claim is used or proved. The dependence on Hohn's U theorem
is a mathematical implication, not an independent verification of his
proof. The proposed large E7-related subgroup is a separate group-lift
question.


1. INPUTS, CONVENTIONS, AND THEOREM DEPENDENCIES

All VOAs and modules are over C. Strong rationality means simplicity,
rationality, C2-cofiniteness, self-contragredience, and CFT type. All
subVOAs below have the same conformal vector as their containing VOA.

The inputs from Hohn [H] are:

  * W is the standard holomorphic c = 32 Barnes-Wall (-1)-orbifold.
    A = O_2(Aut(W)) is elementary abelian of rank 27, and
    Aut(W)/A = E6(2); its action on A is nontrivial irreducible.
  * For 0 != a in A, the unique a-twisted W-module has lowest weight
    rho_a in {1, 3/2, 7/4}.
  * If V = W tensor W, r exchanges its two factors, and
    G = diagonal(A) x <r>, then U = V^G is strongly rational,
    with pointed fusion space D of dimension 56 over F2.
  * Every nonvacuum irreducible U-module has strictly positive weight.
    There is an intrinsic exact sequence

      0 --> E = G* --> D --nu_G--> G --> 0.

    E labels the G-eigenspaces of V. D is hyperbolic, with binary
    quadratic form q(d) = 2 h(U_d) mod 2. Its modular S-matrix is

      S(d,e) = 2^(-28) (-1)^(b(d,e)).

The required extension facts are these standard statements, with exact
primary locations in Section 10:

  (i) A simple VOA extension of a rational pointed VOA is a
      multiplicity-free simple-current extension [S, Proposition 1.1
      and Remark 1.2]. Its support is a subgroup.
  (ii) An isotropic support gives a strongly rational extension; the
       simple modules of that extension have supports gamma + L,
       gamma in L-perp/L. A self-perpendicular support gives a
       holomorphic extension [EMS, Theorem 4.2 and its proof].
  (iii) An automorphism of the base preserving the extension support
        lifts to the extension. The kernel of restriction is the
        character group of the support [S, Theorem 2.1, Corollary 2.2].
  (iv) The type-zero order-two orbifold has hyperbolic four-object
       fusion, and its integral twisted extension is holomorphic when
       the nonvacuum fixed-point modules have positive lowest weights
       [EMS, Theorems 5.13 and 5.16].

We use binary polar forms b, so monodromy is (-1)^b. For an anomaly-free
elementary abelian orbifold of M, let E_K = K* denote its untwisted
charge labels. Then

  0 --> E_K --> D_N --nu_K--> K --> 0,
  (-1)^(b(chi,d)) = chi(nu_K(d)),

and E_K is maximal totally singular. Writing a charge as a label in D_N
is different from using that same character as a dual-grading
automorphism of a transverse extension; both uses are specified below.


2. THE PARTICULAR ITERATED CONSTRUCTION AND ITS COMMON SUBVOA

Number the four W positions 1,2,3,4. Set

  V = W_1 tensor W_2,
  M = V tensor V = W_1 tensor W_2 tensor W_3 tensor W_4,
  B = U tensor U,
  s = (12)(34),
  t = (13)(24),
  Q = diagonal(G) = diagonal(A) x <s>,
  K = Q x <t> = diagonal(A) x P,
  F = M^Q,
  N = M^K = F^t.

Thus dim_F2 G = dim_F2 Q = 28 and dim_F2 K = 29.

Choose identical representatives U_d in the two U positions. Define

  X = direct sum over d in D of U_d tensor U_d.

The diagonal Delta(D) in D direct-sum D is self-perpendicular and
isotropic, so X is a holomorphic strongly rational extension of B.
The literal flip sigma(v tensor w) = w tensor v is a VOA involution:
each product component is a scalar times I_{d,e} tensor I_{d,e}, and
the flip commutes with that tensor map. Fusion multiplicity one and
the tensor-product intertwiner theorem justify this assertion [ADL].

As extensions of B, the supports are

  M: E direct-sum E,
  F: Delta(E),
  X: Delta(D).

The second line follows because equal charge characters are exactly
the pairs whose product is trivial on the diagonal copy of G.
There is an identification of the two copies of the Delta(E)
extension, identity on B, by [S, Theorem 2.1]. This identification
commutes with the factor flip: on each irreducible B-summand
U_e tensor U_e it is a scalar, while both flips are literal exchanges
of the identical factors. Consequently we may and do identify F
as a subVOA of X so that sigma|F is exactly t|F. In particular

  N is a specified subVOA of X^+ = X^sigma.

This is a comparison of extension supports and their actual
identifications. It does not assume that the holomorphic VOAs M and
X lie in a common holomorphic ambient VOA.

Using the local-module quotient by Delta(E), the supports of M and
X as F-extensions are respectively

  (E direct-sum E)/Delta(E),    Delta(D)/Delta(E).

They have trivial intersection, since

  Delta(D) intersect (E direct-sum E) = Delta(E).              (2.1)

This is the transversality statement for the first, Q-gauging step.


3. WHY THE K-FIXED CATEGORY IS POINTED AND POSITIVE

Every element of K has the form (a,p), with a in A acting identically
in all four positions and p in P.

If p = 1 and a != 0, then

  M(a,1) = W(a) tensor W(a) tensor W(a) tensor W(a),
  h(M(a,1)) = 4 rho_a in {4,6,7}.

If p != 1, it is a product of two disjoint transpositions. The
automorphism (a,p) is conjugate to p by inserting a into one endpoint
of each transposition: the product around each two-cycle is a^2 = 1.
Each transposition-twisted W tensor W module has lowest weight
c(W)/16 = 2 and a one-dimensional ground, inherited from W_0 = C.
Therefore

  h(M(a,p)) = 4,
  dim M(a,p)_4 = 1                  for every a and p != 1.    (3.1)

The transposition module and its L0 scaling are the permutation-twist
construction [BDM], also used explicitly in [H, equation (3.10)].

All nontrivial K-twisted modules therefore have positive half-integral
lowest weights. Hohn's elementary-abelian criterion [H, Proposition
3.3] applies to M and K. In particular the K anomaly is zero, N is
strongly rational, every nonvacuum N-module has positive weight, and

  Rep(N) is pointed with hyperbolic fusion space D_N of dimension 58.

For each k = (a,p) with p != 1, let gamma_k in D_N be the unique
simple N-module label containing the line M(k)_4. It is intrinsic:
changing a representative of the unique twisted module does not
change this N-isomorphism class. Equation (3.1) guarantees uniqueness.
Any actual automorphism of M normalizing K transports gamma_k to
gamma_{h k h^(-1)}, since it transports the twisted module and
preserves L0.


4. THE ONLY TWISTED CHARACTER INFORMATION NEEDED HERE

Let chi_d(tau) be the ordinary character of U_d, including the shift
by c(U)/24 = 8/3. The literal flip gives

  Z_X(sigma;tau) = sum_d chi_d(2 tau).

Apply the Fourier S-matrix of D at tau/2. Orthogonality gives

  Z_X(sigma;-1/tau) = 2^28 chi_0(tau/2).                    (4.1)

The twisted modular scalar is exactly +1: [EMS, Proposition 5.5]
states lambda_{0,1} = 1. Thus (4.1) is the actual ordinary character
of X(sigma), not an arbitrarily rescaled trace. Its leading term is
2^28 q^(-4/3). Since c(X)/24 = 16/3, this gives

  h(X(sigma)) = 4,
  dim X(sigma)_4 = 2^28.                                    (4.2)

There is no positivity circularity in this use of Proposition 5.5.
Section 5 of [EMS], through that proposition, assumes the strongly
rational holomorphic CFT-type hypotheses and the finite cyclic
automorphism, but does not assume positive twisted lowest weights.
Its proof first obtains lambda_{0,1}^2 = 1, then fixes the sign by
the positive imaginary-axis ordinary twisted character and the CFT
vacuum limit. Positivity is imposed later to construct the orbifold
extension. Here (4.1) proves the required positive twisted lowest
weight before Theorem 5.16 is used.

The involution has type 4 h(X(sigma)) mod 2 = 0. The nonvacuum
X^+-modules all have positive weights: X^- has no vacuum, and the
two twisted constituents have weights at least 4. Define

  R = X(sigma)_integer,
  Y = X^+ direct-sum R.

The cyclic orbifold theorem supplies its holomorphic VOA structure.
The entire weight-four ground lies in R; it is not halved by taking
the integral constituent. Thus dim R_4 = 2^28. We need no other
coefficient of the character for the preservation argument.


5. TRANSVERSALITY: Y REALLY IS A FULL K-ORBIFOLD

By the pointed-extension result, X and Y are multiplicity-free
simple-current extensions of N. Write

  H_X = support_N(X),
  L_0 = support_N(X^+),
  L = support_N(Y).

The holomorphic supports H_X and L are maximal totally singular
subspaces of D_N, each of dimension 29.

Let chi_Q in K* be the character trivial on Q and negative on K\Q.
Let epsilon in E_K be its untwisted N-module label. Then

  F = N direct-sum N_epsilon,

where t acts negatively on N_epsilon. In particular epsilon is in
H_X, and sigma acts negatively on this summand of X.

Pass from Rep(N) to Rep(F) by epsilon-perp/<epsilon>. The two
images of H_X and E_K are exactly the two F-extension supports in
(2.1). Their intersection is zero. Since both original subspaces
contain epsilon, it follows that

  H_X intersect E_K = <epsilon>.                            (5.1)

The automorphism sigma fixes N pointwise. On each irreducible
N-summand of X it is a scalar by Schur's lemma. The multiplication
of the simple-current extension makes these scalars a character
H_X --> {+1,-1}. It is nontrivial because its value on epsilon is
-1. Consequently

  L_0 = kernel of that character,
  dim L_0 = 28,
  H_X = L_0 direct-sum <epsilon>,
  L_0 intersect E_K = 0.                                    (5.2)

In Rep(X^+) = L_0-perp/L_0, the coset of epsilon is X^-.
The integral twisted module R is the other nonzero isotropic
element of the type-zero order-two hyperbolic fusion group.
If a is any representative of its coset, cyclic fusion gives

  L = L_0 direct-sum <a>,
  b(a,epsilon) = 1.                                         (5.3)

For l in L_0, b(l,epsilon) = 0, since H_X is isotropic. Therefore
every element of a + L_0 has pairing 1 with epsilon. No such element
lies in E_K, because E_K is isotropic and contains epsilon.
Together with (5.2), this proves

  L intersect E_K = 0.                                      (5.4)

The exact twisting-degree sequence now implies that nu_K|L is an
isomorphism L --> K. This is precisely the full-K transversality
claim: Y selects exactly one N-simple constituent from every
physical K-twisted sector, including the vacuum label at degree 1.

Moreover b(epsilon,L_0) = 0 says nu_K(L_0) is contained in Q.
Both have dimension 28, and the restriction is injective, so

  nu_K(L_0) = Q,
  nu_K(a + L_0) = K\Q, each bijectively.                    (5.5)

Thus the R summand consists of exactly 2^28 selected N-simple
modules, one for each degree outside Q. No general theorem asserting
that arbitrary iterated gaugings compose has been assumed; the
actual extension supports prove the required identification here.


6. GROUND SATURATION DETERMINES THIS PARTICULAR SUPPORT

First take k = (a,s), a in A. On V = W tensor W, the element
g_a = (a,a) r is conjugate to the flip r. Its twisted module has
a unique weight-two ground vector v_a. Let F_a be its unique
irreducible U-constituent containing that ground. This is the
module F_a used in [H, Section 4.1].

As a Q-twisted M-module, the degree (a,s) module is
V(g_a) tensor V(g_a). The particular diagonal extension X
contains F_a tensor F_a. The vector v_a tensor v_a has weight
four and is fixed by the literal outer flip sigma.

Here the match with the physical ground is an identification of
N-modules, including the flip eigenlabel. Write d_a for the label
of F_a. The irreducible F-module containing the physical ground
and the irreducible F-module in X containing F_a tensor F_a both
have B-support

  (d_a,d_a) + Delta(E).

By the local-support classification for the Delta(E) extension,
these are the same unique F-module isomorphism class. Choose an
F-module comparison isomorphism using the common F identification
of Section 2. Its restriction to the multiplicity-one irreducible
B-summand F_a tensor F_a is scalar, so it maps the physical ground
line to the X ground line. The physical outer flip on
V(g_a) tensor V(g_a) and sigma on X both implement the same
automorphism of F. Their transported actions on this irreducible
F-module differ by a scalar by Schur's lemma. Both fix the ground
vector v_a tensor v_a, so that scalar is +1. The comparison is
therefore flip-equivariant. In particular the physical ground
belongs to exactly the N-simple label selected in X^+; a character
choice in the common F embedding has not changed its flip
eigenlabel. Consequently

  gamma_(a,s) belongs to L_0, for every a in A.              (6.1)

Now consider the other two nonidentity p in P, namely t and st.
Their degrees constitute exactly K\Q, which has cardinality
2^28. By (5.5), the R summand contains one selected N-simple from
each of those degrees, each with multiplicity one. For any such
degree k, equation (3.1) says that the entire physical twisted
module M(k) has a one-dimensional weight-four space. Therefore
the selected simple contributes either zero or one dimension in
weight four. There are exactly 2^28 selected simples, and
equation (4.2) says their total weight-four dimension is 2^28.
Every term must therefore equal one. Equivalently,

  gamma_(a,t) and gamma_(a,st) belong to L,
  for every a in A.                                         (6.2)

This is a saturation argument for labeled modules. It is stronger
than an ungraded or graded character comparison between VOAs:
the twisting degree identifies each selected simple, its physical
sector supplies an individual upper bound of one, and equality
of the sum forces equality in every sector.

Let

  S_ground = {gamma_(a,p) : a in A, p in P\{1}}.

Equations (6.1)--(6.2) give S_ground subset L. The corresponding
degrees span K. Indeed the degrees (0,p) span P, and, for any
fixed p != 1,

  (a,p) + (0,p) = (a,1)

in additive F2 notation for the group (the displayed 1 denotes
the identity of the multiplicatively written P). Thus the
A-directions also lie in their span. Since nu_K is injective on L,
we conclude

  L = span_F2(S_ground).                                   (6.3)

Equation (6.3) is an intrinsic characterization of the SPECIFIC
support resulting from the literal diagonal-flip construction.
It does not select an arbitrary fixed point in a torsor.


7. ACTUAL FACTOR PERMUTATIONS PRESERVE THE SUPPORT

Every pi in S4 acts as an actual tensor permutation on M,
normalizes K, fixes its diagonal A subgroup, and permutes the
three nonidentity elements of P. Hence it induces an automorphism
of N and transports the intrinsic ground labels by

  gamma_(a,p) --> gamma_(a, pi p pi^(-1)).

It preserves S_ground and therefore L by (6.3). The extension
lifting theorem [S, Theorem 2.1 / Corollary 2.2] gives an
automorphism of Y extending this prescribed automorphism of N.

Important distinction: literal tensor permutations act on M and N.
The proof does not assert that every such permutation acts
literally on X. Identical tensor intertwiners establish the paired
flip on X, while the intrinsic support characterization establishes
the additional permutations on Y.

Similarly h tensor h tensor h tensor h, for h in Aut(W),
normalizes K and sends gamma_(a,p) to gamma_(h a h^(-1),p).
Thus it preserves L and lifts to Y. For h in A, this diagonal
automorphism is already in K and fixes N pointwise. The induced
action on relative extension classes therefore factors through
J = Aut(W)/A = E6(2). The specific Y class is J-fixed. No
splitting of Aut(W) over A has been assumed.

The kernel of restriction Aut_N(Y) is Hom(L,C*) = (Z/2)^29.
Individual lifts of S4 are established. A choice of such lifts
satisfying all S4 relations would require a further splitting
argument and is not claimed here.


8. AN ORDER-THREE LIFT MOVES THE ORIGINAL QUANTUM INVOLUTION

Via the isomorphism nu_K|L, the dual grading group of Y/N is
canonically K*. A character chi acts on its degree-k summand by
chi(k). The original cyclic-orbifold quantum involution z acts
+1 on X^+ and -1 on R. Equation (5.5) identifies it as

  z = chi_Q,

the nonzero P-character whose kernel in K is Q = A x <s>.

Let tau be a three-cycle in S4, for example (123). Its conjugation
cycles the three nonidentity elements of P and also the three
nonzero characters of P*. Any lift g of tau to Y acts on the
grading characters by the corresponding contragredient action.
In particular

  g z g^(-1) = chi_Q composed with tau^(-1) != z.             (8.1)

An exact order-three lift exists without proving an S4 splitting.
Since the restriction of g has order three, g^3 is in the grading
kernel, which has exponent two. Hence g^6 = 1. The element g^4
still restricts to tau, has cube one, and is nontrivial; it has
exact order three. It has the same conjugation action in (8.1).

Thus there is an actual order-three automorphism of Y outside the
centralizer of its original quantum involution. This is a concrete
symmetry enlargement, but it does not identify the full group
generated with any proposed E7-related subgroup as E8(2).

8.1 Conditional location inside the proposed extraspecial centralizer

This subsection assumes the independently audited centralizer result:
restriction from C_Aut(Y)(z) to Aut(X^+) has kernel <z>, and the
preimage Q_0 of the diagonal-extension grading group D* of X is
extraspecial of plus type and order 2^(1+56), with center <z>.
If the separate centralizer description identifies it as O_2 of
the centralizer, use that same Q_0. None of these extraspecial
assertions is proved by the permutation-preservation argument.

The full quantum grading subgroup K* of Y/N lies inside Q_0.
To see this, take chi in K*. On X^+, its restriction acts on
the Q-degree g part by chi|Q(g). Identify Q with G through
the diagonal embedding. The grading character of X over B

  eta_chi(d) = chi|Q(nu_G(d)),       d in D,

has precisely the same restriction to X^+. Indeed, the
F-module of degree g in X is

  direct sum over nu_G(d)=g of U_d tensor U_d,

and the literal flip preserves every summand. Thus the image
of K* in Aut(X^+) is

  Ann_{D*}(E) = {eta : eta|E = 1},

of order 2^28, while the restriction kernel is the two-element
subgroup <chi_Q> = <z>. This proves K* is contained in the
preimage Q_0 of D*.

The subgroup K* is elementary abelian of order 2^29. Under
the extraspecial hypothesis it is maximal elementary abelian:
its image in Q_0/<z> has dimension 28, the maximal totally
singular dimension of the plus-type quadratic space of
dimension 56.

For the order-three lift from this section, put z' = g z g^(-1).
Then

  z' belongs to K*\<z>, hence to Q_0\Z(Q_0).

In particular z is not weakly closed in Q_0 with respect to
Aut(Y): its Aut(Y)-conjugacy class meets Q_0 outside {z}.
This gives a concrete finite-recognition input, conditional on
the separate centralizer theorem. It is not itself a finite
group recognition theorem.


9. TWO SECONDARY CHECKS OF THE J-FIXED / ORDER-THREE ROUTE

These checks are not needed for the stronger ground-saturation
proof, but record independent ways to control the relative origin.

9.1 A direct diagonal-Aut(W) lift preserving the specified base

Let h in Aut(W), and let a be its induced automorphism of U from
h tensor h. The diagonal action a tensor a preserves Delta(D),
so it has a lift to X. On U_d tensor U_d, every such lift is a
scalar times phi_d tensor phi_d, where phi_d is an implementing
intertwiner to the appropriately relabeled U-module. It therefore
commutes with the literal flip sigma.

Its restriction to F and the prescribed h^tensor4 on F are two
lifts of the same automorphism of B. Their ratio is a character
of Delta(E). Every such character extends to Delta(D), so a
grading-character adjustment of the lift on X makes its
restriction to F exactly h^tensor4. Grading characters commute
with sigma, since sigma fixes each diagonal label. The adjusted
lift still commutes with sigma.

It therefore acts on X^+ and preserves the isomorphism class of
the unique sigma-twisted X-module. Preservation of L0 preserves
its integral constituent R. Applying the extension lifting
theorem to X^+ subset Y gives a lift to Y restricting on N to
the prescribed h^tensor4. This proves J-fixity of the actual
relative extension class without choosing a group-coherent
family of lifts.

9.2 The finite affine lemma

The set of transverse isotropic supports relative to E_K is a
torsor under

  H^2(K,C*) = Lambda^2 K*
             = Lambda^2 A* direct-sum (A* tensor P*)
               direct-sum Lambda^2 P*.

Actual normalizers of K act on this torsor with their genuine
group relations; extension uniqueness identifies these supports
with the relevant relative extension classes.

J acts trivially on P and has (A*)^J = 0. Therefore the invariant
difference module is

  (Lambda^2 K*)^J = (Lambda^2 A*)^J direct-sum Lambda^2 P*.

This step needs no semisimplicity in characteristic two: the
mixed summand is just two copies of A* as a J-module.

The order-three tau fixes A, and it acts trivially on the
one-dimensional Lambda^2 P* because every invertible 2-by-2
matrix over F2 has determinant one. Thus tau acts trivially
on the whole J-invariant difference module.

If x is any J-fixed relative extension class, the torsor
difference delta = tau(x) - x is J-invariant because tau and J
commute in the actual action. Then

  0 = tau^3(x) - x
    = delta + tau(delta) + tau^2(delta)
    = 3 delta = delta.

Hence every J-fixed class is tau-fixed. The direct lift in 9.1
supplies J-fixity for this specific Y. The affine lemma alone
would not prove existence of a J-fixed origin. Group relations
only on the linear difference module, without the actual affine
action, would also be insufficient. The ground-saturation proof
does not need either the irreducibility input for J or this lemma.
