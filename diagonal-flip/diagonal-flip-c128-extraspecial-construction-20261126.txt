DIAGONAL EXTENSION, FLIP ORBIFOLD, AND AN ACTUAL EXTRASPECIAL ACTION
Conditional construction at central charge 128
26 November 2026 — plain UTF-8 proof note

STATUS AND SCOPE

This note takes the explicitly stated results about the VOA U in Höhn's
20 November 2026 preprint [H] as input. It does not re-prove that paper.
It derives a strongly rational holomorphic VOA Y of central charge 128,
its character, and an actual subgroup 2_+^(1+56) of Aut(Y). Section 8
separately proves the automorphism/centralizer lemma using the lifting
theorem [Sh]; its module-permutation issue has been independently checked.
Section 9 is conditional on an additional, not supplied, three-cycle.

Unitarity is not asserted. The source [H] states strong rationality, and
the extension/orbifold theorems used below give strong rationality and
holomorphicity. A compatible positive Hermitian form on all extensions
has not been supplied here. Likewise, equality of characters below does
not identify Y with the conditional Barnes–Wall orbifold T_0. No E8(2)
action, group-recognition conclusion, novelty claim, or splitting of an
extension written with a dot is asserted.

All fields are C unless F2 is specified. Put e(x)=exp(2 pi i x). Characters
include the factor q^(-c/24). The letter W below means X^sigma; it is not
the rank-32 VOA denoted W in [H]. Exact source versions and theorem
locations are listed in Section 10.

1. INPUT AND NOTATION

Use the VOA U of [H, Theorem 1.1], with c(U)=64. Its simple modules U_d
are indexed by a nondegenerate plus-type quadratic space

    (D,q_D) = (F2^56,q_+),      q_D(d)=2 h(U_d) mod 2.

Write b(t,d)=q_D(t+d)+q_D(t)+q_D(d). Fusion is addition in D, and

    S_(d,e)=2^(-28) (-1)^b(d,e).

The action of Aut(U)=E7(2) on D is faithful. We only need the following
low-weight data from [H, Theorem 7.2 and (7.6)]:

    U:       dim U_0=1, U_1=0, dim U_2=dim U_3=1,
             dim U_4=dim U_5=141988;
    O_1:     277347807 labels, h(U_d)=2,
             dim (U_d)_2=dim (U_d)_3=1;
    others:  lowest weights 3, 4, 7/2, or 9/2.

The value dim U_5 follows from C_0(q)=C_1(q)+w_0(q^2): the second term
has only even exponents, and the coefficient of q^5 in C_1 is 141988.
Here C_d(q)=q^(8/3) chi_d(tau).

Identify D with its character group through b, and write gamma_t for
the corresponding quantum automorphism. This identification is fixed
throughout; D* in group-shape statements denotes this quantum group.

2. THE DIAGONAL HOLOMORPHIC EXTENSION AND LITERAL FLIP

The subgroup Delta D={(d,d):d in D} in the fusion group D direct-sum D
is isotropic, because 2h(U_d) is an integer, and is self-perpendicular.
Every nonvacuum summand U_d tensor U_d has positive lowest weight.
Theorem 4.2 of [vEMS] therefore gives a strongly rational holomorphic
extension

    X = direct-sum_(d in D) U_d tensor U_d,       c(X)=128.

Choose the same representative U_d in the two positions. Define sigma
on each summand by the literal interchange u tensor v -> v tensor u.
This is an actual order-two VOA automorphism, not just a permutation of
module labels. To see this, choose an intertwiner I_(d,e) for U_d,U_e.
Every projected component of the extended vertex operator is a scalar
multiple of I_(d,e) tensor I_(d,e), since the fusion multiplicity is one.
The interchange commutes with that expression. This is precisely the
local argument used in [H, Section 3.2], now applied to U.

The quantum maps gamma_t act on U_d tensor U_d by (-1)^b(t,d). They
commute with sigma, and form a faithful D*. Moreover sigma is not one
of them: it interchanges omega tensor 1 and 1 tensor omega, whereas
every gamma_t fixes U tensor U pointwise. Thus

    Gamma = D* times <sigma> = F2^56 times F2

acts faithfully on X. Write its elements as (t,epsilon), corresponding
to gamma_t sigma^epsilon.

3. TWISTED CHARACTERS, TYPE, AND THE FLIP ORBIFOLD

On a tensor square the trace of the flip is the character evaluated at
2 tau. Consequently

    Z_X(gamma_t sigma;tau)
      = sum_d (-1)^b(t,d) chi_d(2 tau).

Applying S and the displayed Fourier matrix gives the exact identity

    Z_X(gamma_t sigma;-1/tau) = 2^28 chi_t(tau/2).       (3.1)

Indeed the sum over d is zero unless the transformed module label is t,
and then is 2^56. The usual normalized untwisted-to-twisted S multiplier
is 1, as in [vEMS, Proposition 5.5]. Hence (3.1) is the character of the
unique twisted X-module, not an unidentified scalar multiple. Comparing
the exponent with q^(h-128/24) gives

    h(X(gamma_t sigma)) = 4 + h(U_t)/2.                 (3.2)

In particular the sigma-twisted module M=X(sigma) has lowest weight 4
and ground dimension 2^28. The cyclic type, 4h mod 2, is zero. Choose
the canonical sigma action on M to be exp(2 pi i L_0); its + eigenspace
M^+ consists exactly of the integral-weight vectors. All the ground is
retained. The type-zero theorem [vEMS, Theorem 5.16] gives

    W = X^sigma,          Y = W direct-sum M^+,

a strongly rational holomorphic VOA of central charge 128. Its quantum
involution z acts as +1 on W and -1 on M^+.

For later lifting arguments, the four irreducible W-modules and their
minimum weights are

    W=X^+          0;
    X^-            2;
    M^+            4;
    M^-            11/2.                              (3.3)

For X^-, omega tensor 1 minus 1 tensor omega gives the weight-two vector.
For M^-, (3.1) with t=0 means that a vector of U-weight k contributes
M-weight 4+k/2. Since U_1=0 and U_3 is one-dimensional, the first
half-integral weight is 11/2, not 9/2. The fusion quadratic space of W
is the hyperbolic group of order four: X^- and M^+ are the two nonzero
isotropic classes, and M^- is their anisotropic sum.

4. LOW COEFFICIENTS AND THE ENTIRE CHARACTER

At weights 1,2,3, only U tensor U contributes to W. Therefore Y_1=0
and dim Y_2=dim Y_3=1.

At weight four, the flip-fixed vacuum summand has dimension

    dim U_4 + dim Sym^2(U_2) = 141988+1 = 141989.

Each label in O_1 contributes one symmetric ground tensor, and no other
nonvacuum label contributes. Thus

    dim W_4 = 141989+277347807 = 277489796,
    dim Y_4 = 277489796+2^28 = 545925252.               (4.1)

At weight five, the vacuum contribution is dim U_5+dim(U_2 tensor U_3),
again 141989. Each O_1 label has one flip-fixed tensor of relative level
one, since both its ground and next level have dimension one. The twisted
contribution at weight five is 2^28 dim U_2=2^28. Hence

    dim Y_5 = 545925252.                              (4.2)

Equivalently the exact orbifold character is

    Z_Y(tau) = (1/2) [ sum_d chi_d(tau)^2
                       + sum_d chi_d(2 tau)
                       + 2^28 chi_0(tau/2)
                       + 2^28 e(1/3) chi_0((tau+1)/2) ].    (4.3)

The phase in the final term follows from the action exp(2 pi i L_0)
on M: its twining trace is e(c(X)/24) Z_M(tau+1), and e(16/3)=e(1/3).
In particular it has positive leading coefficient, as required by the
ground action. Formula (4.3) is not needed for the coefficient counting.

The conditional dense coherent Barnes–Wall origin T_0 in the separate
parent work has the same coefficients in weights 0 through 5: 1,0,1,1,
545925252,545925252. This comparison assumes that parent VOA exists;
the construction of Y above does not depend on it. The two holomorphic
characters therefore agree identically. Here is a direct uniqueness
argument. Their difference is holomorphic on the upper half-plane, has
no polar term at infinity, and tends to zero there, because c/24=16/3
and every coefficient through weight five agrees. Its scalar modular
multiplier has order three. Cubing gives a modular function holomorphic
on the compactified modular curve and zero at the cusp, hence zero.
The difference itself is zero.

This is a character identity only. It does not give a VOA isomorphism,
an identification of automorphisms, or an identification of embeddings.

5. THE ANOMALY OF THE ACTUAL ELEMENTARY GROUP Gamma

For the untwisted quantum action alone,

    Z_X(gamma_t;tau) = sum_d (-1)^b(t,d) chi_d(tau)^2,
    Z_X(gamma_t;-1/tau) = sum_u chi_u(tau) chi_(u+t)(tau).  (5.1)

The second equality is again finite Fourier orthogonality. All its
coefficients are nonnegative. Thus the lowest twisted weight is
min_u(h(U_u)+h(U_(u+t))). It is a positive half-integer for t not zero,
so its cyclic type 4h mod 2 is zero. Formula (3.2) gives cyclic type
q_D(t) for gamma_t sigma. Hence the type function on Gamma is

    type(t,epsilon) = epsilon q_D(t).                 (5.2)

All nonidentity twisted minima are positive. Since Gamma is solvable,
V=X^Gamma is strongly rational. The graded twisted-category theorem
[GR, Theorem 3.1] gives

    TwMod_Gamma X = Vec_Gamma^alpha,
    Rep(V) = Z(Vec_Gamma^alpha).

This use is within the proven solvable fixed-point setting; it does not
assume regularity of arbitrary finite-group orbifolds.

Choose a bilinear f:D times D -> F2 with f(t,t)=q_D(t), so that
f(t,u)+f(u,t)=b(t,u). For example in hyperbolic coordinates t=(x,y),
take f((x,y),(x',y'))=y dot x'. Define

    alpha((t,epsilon),(u,eta),(v,zeta))
       = (-1)^(epsilon f(u,v)).                       (5.3)

Bilinearity verifies the normalized 3-cocycle identity. Restricting to
the order-two subgroup generated by (t,epsilon) gives type epsilon
f(t,t), exactly (5.2). The restriction map on H^3 of an elementary
abelian 2-group to all its order-two subgroups is injective [H, Lemma
3.2]. Therefore (5.3) represents the actual Gamma anomaly.

This anomaly is generally nonzero. We do not gauge all of Gamma as an
anomaly-free group. Its restriction to H=<sigma> is trivial; only this
subgroup is gauged in constructing Y.

6. PARTIAL GAUGING: EXPLICIT BIMODULES AND THEIR ASSOCIATOR

Let C=Vec_Gamma^alpha with the representative (5.3), and let
A_H=1 direct-sum sigma be the ordinary group algebra object. Its
multiplication is associative because alpha restricts trivially to H.
The dual category of the corresponding C-module category is the category
of A_H-bimodules with relative tensor product. We compute that category,
including its associator; identifying only its group of simple objects
would not suffice.

For t in D and r in F2, define X_(t,r) with basis x_epsilon in degrees
(t,epsilon), epsilon in F2. Its left sigma action shifts epsilon; its
right sigma action shifts epsilon and multiplies by (-1)^r. The relevant
associators for the bimodule axioms are all 1, since one argument of f
is zero. These are the simple bimodules: a simple has support in one
coset tH, the left action identifies its two homogeneous lines, and the
remaining right action is one of the two characters of H. There are
2^57 such simples.

In X_(t,r) tensor_(A_H) X_(u,s), put w_kappa=x_0 tensor y_kappa.
The balancing relation has no additional associator phase, since
alpha((t,epsilon),(0,1),(u,eta))=1, and gives

    x_epsilon tensor y_eta = (-1)^(r epsilon) w_(epsilon+eta).

The induced left sigma action on w has factor (-1)^(f(t,u)+r): the
left reassociation uses alpha((0,1),(t,0),(u,kappa))^(-1).
The induced right action has factor (-1)^s. On the rescaled basis

    z_kappa=(-1)^(kappa(f(t,u)+r)) w_kappa

the left action is a plain shift and the right label is r+s+f(t,u).
Consequently the simple-object product is

    (t,r)(u,s) = (t+u, r+s+f(t,u)).                   (6.1)

Choose the tensorators just described, with the degree-(t+u,0) vector
x_0 tensor y_0 identified with the corresponding degree-zero basis
vector. On a triple product both parenthesizations carry
x_0 tensor y_0 tensor z_0 to the same canonical vector; the ambient
associator is alpha((t,0),(u,0),(v,0))=1. That vector generates the
simple bimodule under the left H action, so the induced associator is
identically 1 on the whole simple, not just on one selected component.
Bilinearity of f also verifies associativity of (6.1) directly. Thus

    C*_(A_H) is tensor-equivalent to Vec_N,

with no remaining associator twist, where N is the group (6.1). A dual
defined using opposite endofunctor composition can give N^op instead;
our explicit bimodule-product convention gives (6.1), and the group type
and square/commutator forms are unchanged by the opposite convention.

Let z_0=(0,1). Then

    (t,r)^2 = z_0^q_D(t),
    [(t,r),(u,s)] = z_0^b(t,u).

Nondegeneracy of b gives Z(N)=<z_0>, and N/<z_0>=D. Since q_D has plus
type, this is the extraspecial group

    N = 2_+^(1+56).                                  (6.2)

This calculation has been independently checked, including the balancing
sign, rescaling, and trivial associator. It is not an inference from a
matching dimension or a familiar-looking group order.

7. FULL CENTER: WHY N ACTS ON THIS ACTUAL VOA Y

It remains to identify the actual extension Y, not merely an abstract
category with a candidate group N. As a V-module, Y is supported only
in the Gamma-fluxes 1 and sigma: W is in the untwisted sector and M^+
is in the sigma sector, both nonzero. The classification of holomorphic
extensions of a twisted-double theory [EG, Corollary 2 and (3.5)] assigns
them a subgroup K of Gamma and a trivialization of alpha restricted to
K. Its flux support is exactly K. Therefore here K=H. Moreover
H^2(C2,C*)=0, so there is only one such extension class over V once this
subgroup is specified. In the chosen representative it is the full
center of A_H. The required twisted-double identification is supplied
by [GR]; thus the conjectural qualifications of the older VOA discussion
in [EG] are not additional assumptions in this solvable case.

More precisely, let L_Y be the connected commutative algebra object of
Rep(V) representing Y. It is Lagrangian because Y is holomorphic.
By [DMNO, Proposition 4.8 and Remark 4.9], the full-center correspondence
identifies L_Y with the module category C_(A_H), and its category of
modules is the dual category computed in Section 6. Proposition 4.4
then gives an equivalence of the pair, not only of the ambient categories:

    (Rep(V), L_Y)  =  ( Z(Vec_N), I_N(1) ).            (7.1)

Here I_N is right adjoint to the forgetful functor Vec_N's center ->
Vec_N. Under Z(Vec_N), I_N(1) is the regular function algebra Fun(N),
in the symmetric subcategory Rep(N). Right translations of Fun(N)
are N-equivariant algebra automorphisms and give a faithful group N
(up to the harmless opposite convention). Transporting these through
(7.1) gives automorphisms of the algebra object L_Y. The VOA-extension
correspondence carries them to actual VOA automorphisms of the fixed
extension Y acting identically on V. This is the same automorphism step
spelled out in [GR, proof of Theorem 3.2]. It does not require identifying
Y with some other extension having the same character. In particular,

    N <= Aut(Y),             Y^N=V.                 (7.2)

We also need compatibility with the actual quantum involution and the
original D* action. Track the electric character

    chi_c(t,epsilon)=(-1)^b(c,t),      c in D,

which is trivial on H. Its action on the coset bimodule X_(t,r) is the
same scalar (-1)^b(c,t). Hence under the Morita equivalence it becomes
the one-dimensional N-character (t,r) -> (-1)^b(c,t). The V-modules in
W are exactly these 2^56 electric classes, each once: this is the
Gamma-eigenspace decomposition of X with sigma eigenvalue +1. Thus z_0
fixes W pointwise. In the simple-current extension Y=W direct-sum M^+,
an automorphism fixing W is scalar on M^+ by Schur's lemma, and its
square is 1 by the nonzero product M^+ times M^+ -> W. The only two
possibilities are 1 and the quantum involution z. Faithfulness of the
N action shows z_0 is not 1, so z_0=z. Consequently N/<z> acts on W
as the original faithful quantum D*.
This relative tracking is essential; an abstract group order alone
would not identify a preimage subgroup in a later exact sequence.

Finally, the sigma ground in Y has dimension 2^28 and z acts by -1.
The extraspecial group N has one irreducible complex representation on
which z acts by -1, of dimension 2^28. Complete reducibility therefore
makes this ground a single copy of that faithful representation.

8. SEPARATE AUTOMORPHISM AND EXACT-CENTRALIZER LEMMA

This section uses the faithful Aut(U)=E7(2) statement in [H] and the
simple-current lifting theorem [Sh, Theorem 2.1 / Corollary 2.2]. It is
logically separate from the construction and character calculation.
The module-permutation and lifting arguments below have been separately
reviewed. D*.E7(2) denotes an extension; splitting is not assumed.

8.1. The full automorphism group of X.

Because all nonvacuum diagonal modules start at weight at least four,

    X_2 = C omega_1 direct-sum C omega_2,
    (omega_i)_1 omega_j = 2 delta_(i,j) omega_i.

Its two primitive algebra idempotents are omega_1/2 and omega_2/2.
Every automorphism of X preserves this unordered pair, so preserves or
interchanges omega_1 and omega_2. These vectors recover the U factors
intrinsically:

    ker_X L_0^(2) = U tensor 1,
    ker_X L_0^(1) = 1 tensor U.

Indeed a nonzero label has strictly positive weight in both positions,
and in U the zero-weight space is only C1. Thus after composing with
sigma, an arbitrary automorphism restricts to (a,b) in Aut(U)^2.
Preservation of the set of diagonal module pairs forces a and b to have
the same permutation on D. Faithfulness of the action on D forces a=b.

Conversely each diagonal a tensor a preserves the complete set of
simple-current summands. All hypotheses of [Sh, Theorem 2.1] hold:
X is simple, its summands are pairwise inequivalent irreducible modules,
and their fusion is addition in the finite group D. Thus a tensor a
lifts. An automorphism fixing U tensor U pointwise is scalar on each
irreducible summand; compatibility with products makes these scalars a
character of D. Its kernel is exactly D*.

Every such diagonal lift commutes with the literal sigma. On a summand
U_d tensor U_d it is a scalar multiple of S_d tensor S_d, where S_d is
any intertwining isomorphism implementing a from U_d to the appropriate
new U-module. The tensor-product intertwiner space is one-dimensional,
and the same S_d can be used in both positions. Such a map commutes
with the flip. The quantum kernel also commutes with the flip.

Let M_X be the subgroup preserving the two conformal vectors individually.
Then

    1 -> D* -> M_X -> E7(2) -> 1,
    Aut(X) = M_X times <sigma> = (D*.E7(2)) times <sigma>.   (8.1)

The displayed C2 factor is split and central; this says nothing about
whether the extension M_X itself splits.

8.2. Lifting every automorphism of W=X^sigma in both directions.

The four simple W-modules are given in (3.3). Any automorphism of W
preserves their graded characters and hence their lowest weights. Their
three nonvacuum minima 2,4,11/2 are distinct, so every automorphism fixes
each module class. In fact fixing either nonzero isotropic label in the
hyperbolic fusion plane would already fix the other, but the different
minima also establish that their full stabilizer is all of Aut(W).

Apply [Sh, Corollary 2.2] first to X=W direct-sum X^- and then to
Y=W direct-sum M^+. Both are simple C2-graded simple-current extensions
with irreducible inequivalent summands. The restriction homomorphisms
are onto the pointwise stabilizers of their two module classes, and
their kernels are exactly the corresponding quantum C2 groups. Thus

    1 -> <sigma> -> C_Aut(X)(sigma) -> Aut(W) -> 1,
    1 -> <z>     -> C_Aut(Y)(z)     -> Aut(W) -> 1.       (8.2)

By (8.1), C_Aut(X)(sigma)=Aut(X). Hence, canonically through restriction
to W,

    C_Aut(Y)(z)/<z>  =  Aut(W)  =  Aut(X)/<sigma>.       (8.3)

This proves surjectivity rather than assuming an inverse-orbifold
correspondence between all automorphisms. A possible permutation of
the two extension classes is exactly the obstruction removed above.

8.3. Identifying the complete kernel over E7(2).

The actual subgroup N from Section 7 centralizes its center z, and
its quotient acts on W as the D* subgroup of Aut(W). Therefore it is
contained in the preimage of D* under (8.2). That preimage has order
2 times 2^56, equal to |N|, so it is exactly N. Combining (8.1)-(8.3)
gives

    1 -> N -> C_Aut(Y)(z) -> E7(2) -> 1,
    C_Aut(Y)(z) has exact shape 2_+^(1+56).E7(2).       (8.4)

The quotient acts on N/<z> as the specified faithful 56-dimensional
module. Its square form is q_D by Section 6. The centralizer is finite.
No assertion that this centralizer extension splits is required.

9. CONDITIONAL FINITENESS CRITERION; THE THREE-CYCLE REMAINS SEPARATE

Suppose, in addition to the results above, that there is an actual
Klein-four subgroup Q_0={1,z,z_1,z_2} of Aut(Y) and an actual automorphism
cyclically permuting its three nonidentity elements. This is an extra
hypothesis. A prospective permutation of labels, or a prospective S4
normalizer of an ancestor, is not by itself such an automorphism of Y.

Then the three full centralizers C_Aut(Y)(z_i) are conjugate to (8.4)
and finite. C2-cofiniteness implies finite generation of Y: take finitely
many homogeneous representatives of Y/C2(Y), include the vacuum and
conformal vector, and induct on weight in expressions u_(-2)v. Apart
from derivatives involving the vacuum, their positive-weight inputs
have lower weight. Thus the representatives generate the VOA.
By [DG, Theorem 2.4], Aut(Y) is a finite-dimensional affine algebraic
group. Let its complex Lie algebra be a.

For an involution s, Lie C_Aut(Y)(s)=a^s, so the three fixed subspaces
for z,z_1,z_2 are zero. Decompose a into the four characters of Q_0.
Every character of Q_0 has a nonidentity element in its kernel; its
eigenspace is therefore contained in one of these zero fixed spaces.
Hence a=0. A zero-dimensional complex affine algebraic group is finite,
so Aut(Y) is finite under the extra hypothesis.

This proves only the implication. It supplies neither the required
three-cycle nor the preservation/lift of a proposed extension under
S4. It also supplies no finite-group recognition theorem. In particular,
no conclusion Aut(Y)=E8(2) is made here. The separately investigated
Smith recognition source and its precise hypotheses are outside this
frozen note.

REVIEW BOUNDARY

The diagonal/flip construction, coefficient identities, representative
Gamma anomaly, explicit untwisted Vec_N dual, and full-center bridge are
the mathematical content preserved here. The exact centralizer uses the
separate, explicit lifting argument in Section 8. The actual three-cycle,
unitarity, group recognition, and identification with the Barnes–Wall
VOA remain outside these conclusions. No calculation of a finite initial
character segment is used as a substitute for a VOA or group action.
