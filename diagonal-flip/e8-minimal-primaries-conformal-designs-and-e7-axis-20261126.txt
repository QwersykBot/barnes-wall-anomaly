AND AN EXPLICIT E7-CENTRALIZER-FIXED IDEMPOTENT

0. SCOPE AND IMPORTED INPUTS

This note concerns the diagonal-flip construction Y, not an identification of Y
with a Barnes–Wall torsion-origin VOA. No such identification is assumed or
proved. Unitarity is not assumed. Finite-group recognition, the existence of the
VOAs and their extensions, and the published character data are separate inputs.

Let U be the strongly rational building block of central charge 64 in Höhn's
preprint [H64]. Its irreducible modules U_d are indexed by the
nondegenerate plus-type quadratic space D of dimension 56 over F_2. The vacuum
module satisfies

  dim U_0=1, U_1=0, dim U_2=dim U_3=1,
  dim U_4=dim U_5=141988.

There are 277347807 nonzero labels with lowest weight 2 and one-dimensional
lowest space. All other nonvacuum modules have lowest weight at least 3. Form

  X = direct sum over d in D of U_d tensor U_d,

let sigma interchange the factors, and let Y be its type-zero cyclic orbifold.
The frozen construction note [DF] supplies the actual extension, the flip, its
twisted character, and the full quantum-involution centralizer. In particular,

  char X(sigma-twisted) = 2^28 char U(tau/2),

so its lowest conformal weight is 4, with dimension 2^28. The integral twisted
summand of Y contains this whole ground space. Also

  Y_1=0, Y_2=C omega, Y_3=C L_{-3}1,
  dim Y_4=dim Y_5=545925252.

For the weight-six consequence we also use the exact character coefficient

  dim Y_6=76322319758924.

This coefficient follows directly from the low weights just displayed and
modular uniqueness, without another VOA as input. Put f=E4/eta^8 and
j=E4^3/eta^24. Matching the six coefficients in weights 0 through 5 gives

  Z_Y = f [j^5 - 3968 j^4 + 5346369 j^3 - 2798220719 j^2
           + 470623467780 j - 11824040348928].

This expression has the same scalar S and T multipliers as Z_Y, is holomorphic
on the upper half-plane, and has the same polar coefficients. The cube of their
difference is a modular function holomorphic on the compactified modular curve
and zero at its cusp, so the difference vanishes. Expanding E4 and the inverse
Euler products with exact integers gives the displayed weight-six coefficient.
The short, standalone script and receipt [QC] reproduce this expansion from
the six initial coefficients. No character comparison with another VOA or
isomorphism of VOAs is an input to this computation.

Write z for the quantum involution: it fixes X^sigma and acts as -1 on the
twisted extension summand. Write Prim_k(Y) for the Virasoro highest-weight
vectors of weight k, and use the normalized invariant bilinear form (1,1)=1.
The form exists and is nondegenerate for this simple self-contragredient VOA.

The group-theoretic hypothesis for Sections 1–3 is

  Aut(Y) is isomorphic to E8(2).

Write G=Aut(Y) under this hypothesis. Sections 4–7 compute the explicit state and
its spectrum directly in the diagonal-flip construction. They do not use that
recognition hypothesis. The
claim that its line is fixed by the full centralizer uses the exact lifting and
centralizer result in [DF].

1. THE PRIMARY MODULES THROUGH WEIGHT SIX

At central charge 128 the vacuum Virasoro spaces in weights 4 and 5 each have
dimension 2. Their Gram forms are nonsingular. Since there are no lower positive
primary weights, the weight-four decomposition is

  Y_4 = Vir_vac,4 direct sum Prim_4(Y),
  dim Prim_4(Y) = d = 545925250.

The complete z-negative twisted ground is contained in Prim_4(Y): positive
Virasoro modes preserve the twisted summand, which has no states below weight 4.
Consequently the action of E8(2) on Prim_4(Y) is nontrivial.

The published complete complex character-degree list [L], checked in [CS], has
minimum nontrivial degree d, occurring once. Maschke's theorem now implies that
Prim_4(Y) is that unique irreducible module: any nontrivial constituent already
uses the entire dimension. Its action is faithful because E8(2) is simple and
the action is nontrivial. This argument uses the minimum among all ordinary
complex character degrees, not merely among unipotent characters.

For p in Prim_4(Y), L_1 L_{-1}p=8p. Hence L_{-1} is injective on Prim_4(Y), and
its image has zero intersection with the vacuum Virasoro space. Comparing
dimensions gives

  Y_5 = Vir_vac,5 direct sum L_{-1} Prim_4(Y),
  Prim_5(Y)=0.

These low-degree decompositions do not require a global decomposition of Y into
irreducible Virasoro modules or a positive-definite Hermitian form.

The same argument extends to weight 6 with one further character constraint.
The vacuum Virasoro space has dimension 4. For a primary p of weight 4, the
Gram matrix of its two level-two descendants (L_{-2}p,L_{-1}^2 p), relative to
(p,p), is

  [[80,24],[24,144]],   determinant 10944 != 0.

Thus Y_6 decomposes, as a G-module, into four trivial vacuum descendants, two
copies of Prim_4(Y), and Prim_6(Y). In particular

  a := dim Prim_6(Y)
     = 76322319758924 - 4 - 2*545925250
     = 76321227908420.

The actual quantum involution z gives a second independent constraint. Its
negative part in Y_6 is the integral sigma-twisted space of that weight. The
twisted character above gives

  dim (Y_6)^- = 2^28 dim U_4 = 38114613526528,
  Tr_{Y_6}(z) = 76322319758924 - 2*38114613526528
              = 93092705868.

Likewise its primary-weight-four trace is obtained from the actual twisted
ground, not an assumed conjugacy-class label in E8(2):

  chi_4(z) = 545925252 - 2*2^28 - 2 = 9054338.

Consequently

  chi_6(z) = 93092705868 - 4 - 2*9054338 = 93074597188.

The complete degree census in [CS, Section 3] states that the only irreducible
degrees <=a are 1,d,a, each occurring once in the published degree list. If the
degree-a irreducible did not occur in Prim_6(Y), write its representation as
x copies of the trivial module and y copies of Prim_4(Y), with x,y nonnegative
integers. Dimension and the trace at this same actual z would imply

  x + y*d = a,
  x + y*9054338 = 93074597188.

Subtracting forces

  y=141986,
  x=a-141986*d=-1192514638080 < 0,

a contradiction. The unique degree-a irreducible therefore occurs and exhausts
the dimension. Prim_6(Y) is irreducible and nontrivial (hence faithful). No
published value of that larger character at z was needed to force this result.

2. EVERY HOMOGENEOUS SPACE IS A CONFORMAL SIX-DESIGN

Höhn's definition [HD, Section 2, printed p. 7] requires, for a homogeneous
module space Z and every v of weight at most t,

  Tr_Z o(v) = Tr_Z o(pi(v)),

where pi projects onto the vacuum Virasoro component and o(v)=v_{wt(v)-1}.
Theorem 2.5 on printed p. 8 obtains this condition from a compact automorphism
group whose fixed-point VOA has no positive Virasoro primary weight at most t.

Here one can prove the required finite-degree instance directly. Put G=E8(2).
Section 1 shows that, for 0<=k<=6,

  (Y_k)^G = Vir_vac,k.

For any n, trace on Y_n is G-invariant. Average v over the finite group. On the
explicit decompositions above this Reynolds average is exactly pi(v). Thus

  Tr_{Y_n} o(v) = Tr_{Y_n} o(average_G v) = Tr_{Y_n} o(pi(v)).

Every homogeneous space Y_n is therefore a conformal 6-design based on Y. This
uses actual finite-group automorphisms, so compactness is automatic and no
unitarity assumption is needed. It is an assertion for every n, not only n=4.
In particular these are conformal 5-designs as well. No failure of a higher
design condition has been established; no weight-seven claim is made here.

3. THE PRIMARY PRODUCT AND THE IMPORTED CHARACTER CALCULATION

Define, on Prim_4(Y),

  u circle v = pr_Prim4(u_3 v).

The projection is along the vacuum Virasoro space in Y_4; it is orthogonal for
the invariant bilinear form and commutes with every automorphism. Skew symmetry
implies commutativity after this projection, since the correction terms are
Virasoro derivatives, orthogonal to primaries. For primary u of weight 4,
invariance of the bilinear form makes u_3 self-adjoint. Thus

  (u circle v,w) = (u,v circle w)

and the associated trilinear form is symmetric and G-invariant.

The frozen exact character screen [CS] supplies the additional statement

  dim (Sym^3 Prim_4(Y))^G = 1.

It derives this from the published minimal character, its tensor-cube invariant
dimension, the complete character-degree list, and symmetric/exterior-square
constraints. Those character calculations are imported here, not repeated.
Section 4 below proves that the VOA trilinear form is nonzero. It therefore
realizes this unique invariant cubic up to a nonzero scalar, equivalently the
unique nonzero G-equivariant commutative multiplication up to scalar, after
using the invariant bilinear form. No associativity, Jordan identity, complete
fusion law, or identification with a separately constructed algebra is claimed.

4. AN EXPLICIT TWO-FACTOR PRIMARY AND ITS NONZERO CUBIC

For the calculation, allow the common factor central charge to be a variable c;
its value in the construction is 64, while the diagonal central charge is 2c.
Let L_n^(1),L_n^(2) be the commuting Virasoro modes on U tensor U, and set

  A = ((L_{-2}^(1))^2 + (L_{-2}^(2))^2)1,
  B = (L_{-4}^(1) + L_{-4}^(2))1,
  C = L_{-2}^(1)L_{-2}^(2)1.

All three states are flip-even. The symbol C in this section denotes this
state, not an automorphism centralizer. Define

  p = A - (3/5)B - [2(5c+22)/(5c)] C.

Here and below c is nonzero. The formula is used at c=64, where none of the
displayed Gram factors vanish. To check primarity, let omega=omega_1+omega_2.
The Virasoro commutation relations give

  L_1 A = 3(L_{-3}^(1)+L_{-3}^(2))1,
  L_1 B = 5(L_{-3}^(1)+L_{-3}^(2))1,   L_1 C=0;
  L_2 A = (c+8)omega,   L_2 B=6omega,   L_2 C=(c/2)omega.

Thus L_1 p=L_2 p=0. These two modes generate all positive Virasoro modes, so p
is primary. The Gram matrix on the ordered basis (A,B,C) is

  G = [[c(c+8), 6c, 0],
       [6c,     10c, 0],
       [0,      0,   c^2/4]].

The following three matrices give zero modes restricted to the flip-even
weight-four span of (A,B,C). Columns are images of the ordered basis vectors:

  o(A) = [[40+2c, 12,     0],
          [12+6c, 44+10c, 0],
          [0,     0,      16+2c]],

  o(B) = 12 I,

  o(C) = [[0,     0,  c/2],
          [3c,    5c, 0],
          [2c+16, 12, 4]].

These are small universal Virasoro calculations, not character-table input.
For example, for a single factor,

  o(L_{-2}^2 1) = L_0^2 + 2L_0 + 2 sum_{n>=1} L_{-n}L_n,

and the cross-factor zero mode is

  o(C) = sum_{n in Z} L_n^(1)L_{-n}^(2).

Also B=(1/2)L_{-1}^2 omega, so o(B)=3L_0, equal to 12 on weight 4.

Put V_2=(L_{-2}^(1)+L_{-2}^(2))^2 1=A+2C and V_4=B. Matrix multiplication gives

  (p,p) = 2(5c+11)(5c+22)/25,

  p_3 p = [4(5c^2+33c-44)/(5c)] p
          + [84(5c+22)/(25c)] V_2
          + [12(c-2)(5c+22)/(25c)] V_4.

As an independent normalization check, the displayed expression satisfies

  (p_3 p,V_4)=12(p,p),   (p_3 p,V_2)=24(p,p),

as follows directly by commuting L_4 or L_2^2 past p_3.

At c=64 take the integral normalization

  P = 80p = 80A - 48B - 171C.

Then

  (P,P) = 57959424,
  P_3 P = 22548 P + 114912 V_2 + 1017792 V_4,
  P circle P = 22548 P,
  (P circle P,P) = 1306869092352 != 0.

This is a direct nonzero witness in X^sigma, which is an actual subVOA of Y.
It is independent of the group-recognition result and of any Y versus T_0
isomorphism. In particular

  e = P/22548

is an idempotent for the primary product.

5. FIXED LINE AND THE FOUR EIGENVALUES

The exact-centralizer construction in [DF] identifies

  C_Aut(Y)(z)/<z> = Aut(X)/<sigma>,
  Aut(X) = (D^* . E7(2)) times <sigma>.

The quantum D^* fixes the vacuum tensor component pointwise, the diagonal E7(2)
fixes both conformal vectors, and the flip fixes A,B,C. Their actual lifts to Y
therefore fix P. Thus the line C P is fixed pointwise by the full centralizer
C_Aut(Y)(z). This assertion does not by itself claim that it is the entire
centralizer-fixed subspace of Prim_4(Y).

There are four direct vector-space summands of Prim_4(Y), with the following
eigenvalues for the operator u -> P circle u:

  summand                         dimension       eigenvalue
  C P                                      1          22548
  diagonal copy of Prim_4(U)           141986           1344
  weight-(2,2) ground spaces        277347807             20
  sigma-twisted ground              268435456         -171/8

These dimensions sum to 545925250. This lists vector-space eigenspaces; it does
not claim an irreducible decomposition under the centralizer.

For the second and third rows, an ordinary tensor Virasoro highest-weight
vector of weights (h_1,h_2) has P zero-mode eigenvalue

  80(h_1^2+h_2^2) + 16(h_1+h_2) - 171 h_1 h_2.

Substitute (4,0) or (0,4), and (2,2), respectively. These states are already
diagonal primaries. The first row is Section 4. Section 6 proves the fourth
directly, without deducing it from a character or vanishing trace.

Consequently multiplication by e=P/22548 is diagonalizable on Prim_4(Y), with
four distinct eigenvalues and the same ordered multiplicities:

  1,   112/1879,   5/5637,   -57/60128.

The 1-eigenspace is one-dimensional. Calling e an idempotent with this spectrum
does not assert an unproved axial fusion law.

6. DIRECT PERMUTATION-TWISTED NORMAL-ORDERING CALCULATION

Keep c for the central charge of one factor. The two-cycle twisted stress
fields can be written using the covering coordinate t=+sqrt(z) or -sqrt(z):

  T_1(z) = (4z)^(-1) T(+sqrt(z)) + c/(32z^2),
  T_2(z) = (4z)^(-1) T(-sqrt(z)) + c/(32z^2).

This formula can be checked directly by reindexing the integer modes of T_1+T_2
and the half-integer modes of T_1-T_2. The covering modes have central charge c,
and their zero mode satisfies

  L_0^cover = 2 L_0^twisted - c/8.

The twisted ground has weight c/16 (equal to 4 at c=64), hence covering weight
zero. Positive
covering modes annihilate the whole ground space. The Virasoro commutation
relations then determine ground-to-ground products of stress fields as scalars,
even when that ground space has multiplicity 2^28. No assumption that the
ground is one-dimensional is involved.

Write Omega for this entire ground space. Projecting the two stress products
to Omega gives the following scalar kernels times the identity of Omega:

  same branch:
    c/[32 z w (sqrt(z)-sqrt(w))^4] + c^2/[1024 z^2 w^2];

  opposite branches:
    c/[32 z w (sqrt(z)+sqrt(w))^4] + c^2/[1024 z^2 w^2].

These follow from the covering vacuum stress kernel c/[2(t-s)^4] and the two
Schwarzian terms. The reindexed positive-mode commutators also prove the same
formulas directly on every vector of Omega.

For A, the correct subtraction is the ordinary same-factor VOA OPE

  (c/2)/(z-w)^4 + 2 T_i(w)/(z-w)^2 + partial T_i(w)/(z-w),

not a new twisted-normal-ordering convention. Write z=w+u. The non-Schwarzian
same-branch scalar has expansion

  c/(2u^4) + c/(16w^2 u^2) - c/(16w^3 u)
  + 31c/(512w^4) + O(u).

After projection back to Omega, T_i(w) has value c/(32w^2) times the identity,
so exactly the first three terms are subtracted.
The regular term for one squared stress is c(c+62)/(1024w^4). Summing the two
factors gives

  o(A)|Omega = c(c+62)/512.

There is no ordinary singular cross-factor OPE. Taking the regular diagonal
limit of the opposite-branch kernel gives

  o(C)|Omega = c(c+2)/1024.

Finally B=(1/2)partial^2(T_1+T_2), and the twisted conformal weight is h=c/16,
so

  o(B)|Omega = 3h = 3c/16.

At c=64 these values are

  A: 63/4,   B: 12,   C: 33/8.

Therefore, on the complete ground space,

  o(P) = 80(63/4) - 48(12) - 171(33/8) = -171/8.

For the c-dependent normalized primary p of Section 4, the same calculation
simplifies to o(p)|Omega=-(5c+22)/1280. All conclusions here use ordinary VOA
OPE regularization. The derivative contribution is 3h, not 6h.

7. AN INDEPENDENT TRACE CHECK

The three untwisted primary summands contribute

  22548 + 1344*141986 + 20*277347807
    = 5737807872
    = 171*2^25.

The twisted ground contributes (-171/8)*2^28=-171*2^25. Thus

  Tr_Prim4(Y) o(P) = 0.

Here Tr_Prim4(Y) o(P) means Tr(pr_Prim4(Y) ∘ o(P)|_Prim4(Y)), the compressed
trace; the raw zero mode o(P) need not preserve Prim4(Y).

Since P is primary, the trace on the weight-four vacuum Virasoro summand is
also zero (equivalently, its diagonal matrix elements vanish by Virasoro
invariance). Thus the same cancellation holds on Y_4. This equality was checked
from the direct untwisted and twisted calculations, not used to infer the
twisted eigenvalue. It is consistent with the design consequence of recognition.
