2. THE SAME F EMBEDDING AND TRANSVERSALITY

Over U tensor U, the supports of M,F,X are E+E, Delta E, Delta D.
The two Delta E subextensions are isomorphic by simple-current
uniqueness, identically on U tensor U. On each multiplicity-one
U_e tensor U_e summand that isomorphism is scalar, so it commutes
with the literal flips. Thus F is identified inside X with precisely
sigma|F=t|F; this specifies the SAME B inside Xplus.

Their F-extension supports have trivial intersection, because

  Delta D intersect (E+E) = Delta E.

Let H=support_B(X), L0=support_B(Xplus), and L=support_B(Y).
Let epsilon be the electric label of the character chi_Q of K, trivial
on Q and negative on K minus Q. Then F=B plus B_epsilon. Passing to
epsilon-perp/<epsilon> shows

  H intersect E_K = <epsilon>.

The literal sigma is negative on B_epsilon. Hence its character on
H cuts H=L0 direct-sum <epsilon>, with L0 intersect E_K=0.
The integral cyclic neighbor has

  L=L0 direct-sum <a>,       b(a,epsilon)=1.

Every a+L0 vector has pairing 1 with epsilon, while every E_K vector
has pairing 0. Thus L intersect E_K=0, nu|L is an isomorphism L->K,
and nu maps L0 bijectively onto Q and L minus L0 onto K minus Q.

This identifies the particular Y as a full K-orbifold class. It is the
essential interface for both preservation proofs. A value of dim Y4
alone would not establish it.

Consequently Y contains the intrinsic ground-bearing B-label gamma_k

4. S4 PRESERVATION AND AN ACTUAL ORDER-THREE LIFT

Every literal factor permutation normalizes K and transports gamma_k
to gamma_(pi k pi^-1), preserving the preceding set and hence L.
Simple-current lifting therefore gives an actual automorphism of Y
extending each such base automorphism. The same argument applies to
diagonal Aut(W32).

P itself acts trivially on B, so this is not a claim of a faithful
literal S4 action on B, or of a coherent S4 section in Aut(Y). A
three-cycle has nontrivial order-three restriction: it cyclically
moves the three nonidentity permutation fluxes and their distinct
ground-bearing B-labels.

The quantum kernel Aut_B(Y) is Hom(L,C*)=K*, elementary abelian of
order 2^29. If g is any lift of the chosen three-cycle, g^3 belongs
to this kernel, so g^6=1. Then h=g^4 still restricts to the same
three-cycle and has exact order 3. No splitting theorem is needed.

The original quantum z equals chi_Q because its + part has degrees
Q and its - part has degrees K minus Q. Conjugation by h acts
contragrediently on K*, cycling the three nonzero P* characters.
Thus an ACTUAL Klein four P* contains z and its two moved conjugates.

6. ROOT'S WEIGHT-FOUR MAXIMUM ARGUMENT: PASS WITH ITS INTERFACES

This is an independent preservation argument AFTER section 2 has placed
the same Y among full K-orbifolds. It does not replace that placement.

Untwisted contribution. Höhn's F_0 character gives dim(W32_4^A)=2484.
At total weight 4, single-factor contributions form one P-orbit. The
six pairs of weight-two factors form three P-orbits. In W32_2 the
trivial A-character and each of the 139503 nontrivial characters have
multiplicity 1. Each A-invariant tensor square is therefore fixed by
its pair swap. Consequently

  dim B4=2484+3(139503+1)=420996.

Nontrivial permutation degrees. There are 3*2^27 such degrees, each
with ground dimension 1, so their selected contributions are at most
3*2^27.

Pure diagonal degrees. Only the 139503 rank-one a have minimum 4.
The a-twisted W32 ground Omega_a has dimension 32. The elementary-abelian
cyclic detector identifies its A anomaly with the Albert cubic N.
The commutator of the slant at a is the polar form of the quadratic
derivative. At a=(E11,0,0), the latter is

  x22*x33+x23*x32+y11*z11+y12*z21+y13*z31.

These are five disjoint hyperbolic pairs, so the commutator rank is 10.
This exact quadratic also appears in Höhn Lemma 4.4/(4.8). Every
irreducible projective A-representation for this multiplier has
dimension 2^5=32. Hence Omega_a is one irreducible such representation.

Its trace vanishes outside the 17-dimensional commutator radical,
and the radical acts scalarly. After a choice of linearization,
Omega_a^tensor4 is an ordinary A-representation whose character has
support on that radical. Each extending A-character occurs with
multiplicity 32^4/2^10=1024; all others occur with multiplicity 0.
A different linearization only translates the character labels.
Refining by the commuting P action cannot increase a single selected
K-character multiplicity. The bound is therefore 1024 per rank-one
degree, not merely a dimension estimate without a multiplicity proof.

Adding these pointwise bounds gives, for EVERY full K-orbifold,

  dim weight 4 <= 420996 + 3*2^27 + 139503*1024
              = 545925252.

The previously constructed Y attains this number. Any other transverse
support attaining it must contain the unique ground label for EVERY
nontrivial permutation degree. Those labels span the already identified
L, so that support equals L. Equivalently its torsion difference B has
zero slant on a spanning set of K and hence B=0.

Thus Y is the unique maximum relative full-K extension class, which
also proves preservation under normalizer transport. This is uniqueness
within that specified relative orbifold problem, not uniqueness of all
c=128 VOAs with the same character.
