The proposed argument, code and exposition in this packet were generated by coordinated AI agents. One agent developed the compatible quaternion-lift route and Cartesian calculation. A separate agent reconstructed the matrices directly from the source formulas, checked all quadratic lift phases, and supplied the analytic involution-standardness argument. A subsequent block-cocycle argument replaced the finite lift calculation as the logical justification of the specific quaternion compatibility step; two other agents checked its character argument, Gram divisibility and integral row formula independently. This separation is useful internal evidence; it is not external expert or human validation.

Keller, Ashley Winter Roberts and Jeremy Roberts supplied the Barnes–Wall action, the conditional construction, the twisted-module weights and the low-weight spectral analysis. Johnson–Freyd and Treumann supplied the extraspecial-group cohomology filtration used here. In particular, their proof concerning the sporadic group J4 already uses a quaternion subgroup inside a binary dihedral group to eliminate a cohomology class (printed page 30). The general idea of quaternion restriction is therefore prior work. The potentially new step here is its realization by compatible lattice-VOA lifts for the **specified** KRR action; the draft makes no general-method priority claim.

## Standardness issue and its independent repair

The literal statement that any tensor word containing a Y=XZ factor has trivial fixed lattice is too broad: Y1Y2 has a 64-dimensional real fixed space. Inspection of the KRR source ancillary `BWOrbifolds.nb` found that the final loops use `Tuples[{0,1,2},6]` for the remaining factors and therefore do not cover Y factors there. Those observations do not disprove the intended standardness conclusion.

Section 3 proves that conclusion independently. It derives the two nonzero generator phase quadratics from the half-Gram matrix, uses their invariance under the binary generator actions, and applies a six-variable exterior-algebra identity to show vanishing on the **entire binary fixed space** of every involution. Integral fixed vectors reduce to that space, so no unproved saturation assertion about a computed nullspace is needed. The source phase identifications are also checked coefficient by coefficient by both lift implementations. The optional exhaustive checker confirms the finite-dimensional lemma independently of the analytic derivation.

The quaternion plane condition is similarly explicit: both vectors have quadratic value one **and** their polar pairing is one. Nonsingularity of the two vectors alone would not suffice.

## What has and has not been checked

Section 4 supplies an analytic proof of the lift compatibility. The half-Gram form vanishes modulo two within each of sixteen eight-dimensional coordinate blocks. All required isometries preserve those blocks, and two genuine lifts of the same isometry differ by a lattice character. These facts force the canonical lifts to form a homomorphism on the relevant subgroup. A two-term row formula proves integrality of the order-eight generator directly. The finite code is therefore corroboration, not a logical dependency of that step.

The primary checker uses arbitrary-precision integer arithmetic, verifies the cocycle-lift identity, and checks every coefficient of the quadratic phase differences for the four stated relations. This is a complete finite check of those identities; it is not a sample of lattice vectors. The alternative code starts in Cartesian coordinates, converts through the lattice basis and reaches matching matrix hashes without importing the primary implementation. Its NumPy signed-integer arithmetic is secondary evidence; the arbitrary-precision checker is the minimal finite certificate.

The cohomology, standardness, block-lift, anomaly naturality and VOA extension steps remain mathematical arguments requiring reading and assessment. They have not been encoded in a formal proof kernel. The reports do not establish a generic theorem about Barnes–Wall dimensions, the full normalizer, a unitary extension or uniqueness. The exact-primary-gap argument uses the kernel of L1 and L2 on invariant weight-four Heisenberg states, so it does not implicitly assume unitarity.

The finite code contains no generic inhomogeneous linear-system solver. The exploratory solver and large search outputs were omitted rather than added to the proof's trusted components.

## Current-literature and duplicate-resolution check

The independent search was performed on **26 November 2026**, including recent-result filters and combinations of “Barnes-Wall”, “anomaly”, “Conjecture 5.2”, “Keller Roberts”, “quaternion”, “Q16”, “Q_16”, “vanishing”, “resolution” and “holomorphic gap 4”. Primary pages and papers were inspected where a potentially overlapping result appeared. No matching resolution or compatible Q16 lift argument was located in the checked sources. Absence from this bounded search is not a priority result.

Three primary status observations matter:

1. The [published KRR article](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP04(2026)009), dated 1 April 2026, still states the anomaly-vanishing conjecture and the conditional central-charge-128 construction.
2. Ji and Tian, [*CFTs with Large Virasoro Primary Gaps*, arXiv:2611.23231v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23231v1), 17 November 2026, prove a related full, nonchiral construction. Their Theorem 6.1 inflates the chiral anomaly and cancels it on the left/right fiber-product symmetry. That does not establish that the individual chiral anomaly vanishes. This preprint is context only, not a dependency of the present proof.
3. Höhn, [*An Extremal Vertex Operator Algebra of Central Charge c=64*, Hexagon 2611.00631v2](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00631v2), 19 November 2026, claims a different central-charge-64 construction. Its §1.2 explicitly describes the KRR central-charge-128 construction as conditional on anomaly vanishing. We have not independently validated Höhn's full construction and use it only as contemporary status and overlap evidence.

These related results make “first CFT with gap above two” and similarly broad priority language inappropriate. The proposed result is the specified chiral anomaly statement and its stated orbifold consequence.
